Who We Are

A trusted partner for integrated technology & infrastructure.

Recent technological advances have driven massive economic transformations and disruptions worldwide. Quietly observing this dynamic landscape from one corner of Bangladesh, Avant Technologies has emerged as a pioneer in surveillance systems and integrated solutions.

Founded in 2015 in Dhaka, the capital of Bangladesh, Avant Technologies began its journey as a technology-oriented firm. Over the years, it has firmly established its presence in the country's technological sector, offering high-tech products and superior security solutions and services.

The company has successfully collaborated with key government bodies, including the Bangladesh Police, Bangladesh Army, Special Security Force (SSF), Dhaka North City Corporation (DNCC), Dhaka South City Corporation (DSCC), Sylhet City Corporation (SCC), Power Grid Bangladesh PLC (PGCB), the Ministry of Home Affairs (MOHA), Border Guard Bangladesh (BGB), and Ansar & VDP. Avant Technologies has also partnered with leading financial institutions, providing clients with a comprehensive range of cybersecurity, surveillance systems, and software solutions.
